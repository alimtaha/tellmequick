# Demo script — Investment meeting: "Should we buy COIN?" (~1:45)

A natural back-and-forth between **Sarah Kim** (PM) and **Alex Rivera** (analyst). They're
genuinely thinking out loud, hitting gaps ("what was that number again…", "let me pull it
up"), and the agent **"tellmequick"** only chimes in to fill the gap with the receipt — it's
not being fed clean questions. Talk over each other, leave pauses; let the agent earn each
interjection. ~230 spoken words.

*Tip for the live run: the agent interjects right after someone trails off or admits they're
unsure — that hesitation is the cue. Don't rush to the next line; give it the beat.*

---

**Sarah:** "Okay… Coinbase. I keep going back and forth on this one."

**Alex:** "Same. Didn't we already look at it a few weeks back and pass?"

**Sarah:** "We did, yeah. I don't totally remember the reasoning though…"

**🤖** "You passed at the May 15 deal review — waiting on the print and the restructuring. The note said a 1% starter cap if you did buy." *(meetings + slack)*

**Sarah:** "Right, that was it. So… has anything actually changed since then?"

**Alex:** "I mean, the big one is the layoffs."

**Sarah:** "Right, how big was that again? I saw the headline but I don't have the number in front of me."

**Alex:** "Uh, hang on, let me pull it up—"

**🤖** "About 700 people — roughly 14% of staff — and a $50 to $60 million charge. They framed it as restructuring for the AI era." *(8-K)*

**Alex:** "Yeah. Honestly that reads like discipline to me, not a company in trouble."

**Sarah:** "Maybe. But if they're cutting that hard… are the execs themselves bailing? That'd worry me."

**Alex:** "I looked at the Form 4s earlier… I don't really remember the details, though."

**🤖** "No real exit. The CFO's $2 million sale was a scheduled 10b5-1 plan, and the COO's was just tax withholding on a stock vest." *(Form 4 + slack)*

**Sarah:** "Okay, that actually helps. And who else is in it — is this just retail money?"

**Alex:** "No, it's real… the big index names, and ARK I think?"

**🤖** "Vanguard 7.4%, BlackRock 5.7%, Jane Street 5.3%, ARK 5.1% — about a quarter of the float." *(SC 13G)*

**Sarah:** "That's a real base. Last thing nagging me — what's their actual AI product plan? Everyone keeps saying 'AI era.'"

**Alex:** "…yeah, I've got nothing there. It's not really in the filings."

**🤖** *(stays quiet — nothing to back it up)*

**Sarah:** "Then we flag it as unknown. We don't guess on the memo."

**Sarah:** "Alright — insiders aren't leaving, smart money's in, the cuts look like discipline. I'm comfortable starting small. Let's do a 1% position."

---
*The interjections are earned by real gaps (hesitation, "let me pull it up", silence), not
clean setup questions. Cross-index proof, memory recall (beat 1), real filing numbers, and a
no-bluff empty state.*
